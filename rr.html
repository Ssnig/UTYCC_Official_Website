Rules and Regulations

  • 1. Peaceful Learning Environment
    • (1)Students must not directly or indirectly disrupt or cause harm to the peaceful learning environment.

  • 2. Prohibition of Moral Misconduct
    • (1)Students are strictly prohibited from using, possessing, buying, selling, or distributing narcotics. Smoking cigarettes or chewing betel nut in classrooms, workshops, and within the campus premises is also strictly forbidden.
    • (2)Students must not say or do anything that could harm the Rector, Pro-Rector, teachers, or staff.
    • (3)Students must not speak, joke, or act in a way that could harm or harass female students or other women, whether on or off campus.
    • (4)Gambling, drinking alcohol, or using intoxicants are absolutely prohibited, both on and off campus.
    • (5)Students must obey the instructions and guidance of their teachers.
    • (6)Students must not destroy, steal, or forcibly take the property of others, whether on or off campus.
    • (7)Fighting among students or with any outside individual is not permitted, on or off campus.
    • (8)Students must adhere to the rules and regulations set by the university/college/institute. This includes their behavior and speech, even outside of school hours.
    • (9)Students are not allowed to bring any dangerous weapons into the school.

  • 3. Strict Adherence to School Discipline
    • (1)All students must attend classes regularly.
    • (2)To be eligible to take the final exam, a student must have a minimum of 75% class attendance throughout the academic year. Students with less than 75% attendance will not be allowed to take the final exam.
    • (3)Students must abide by the rules and regulations set by the school and for their specific courses.

  • 4. Compliance with Exam Regulations
    • (1)All students must take the mid-term exam without fail. If a student misses the mid-term exam, they will not be allowed to take the final exam.
    • (2)Cheating or using any dishonest means during an exam will result in severe action. Students must not engage in any form of academic misconduct.

  • 5. Dress Code and Conduct
    • (1)Students must wear the designated uniform and behave and speak in a manner appropriate for a student.

  • 6. Simultaneous Enrollment
    • (1)While attending this university of technology, students are not permitted to be simultaneously enrolled in any other university, college, or institute. If a student is found to be in violation of this rule, their name will be removed from the student roster.


    • Penalties for Violation

    • Upon a thorough reading and understanding of the above-mentioned points, any failure to comply with or violation of these rules will result in disciplinary action appropriate to the severity of the offense.
