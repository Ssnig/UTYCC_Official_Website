Precision Engineering (PrE)

Program Overview

Precision Engineering (PrE) is a dynamic field focusing on the design, analysis, and testing of aircraft, spacecraft, and other mechanical systems. This program combines principles of mechanical engineering with specialized aerospace topics such as aerodynamics, propulsion, flight mechanics, and structural design. Students are prepared to contribute to the next generation of aerospace innovation.

Career Opportunities

  • Reservoir Engineer: Optimize oil and gas recovery from reservoirs.
  • Drilling Engineer: Design and manage drilling operations.
  • Production Engineer: Maximize efficiency of oil and gas production.
  • Petrophysicist: Analyze rock and fluid properties of reservoirs.
  • Geologist (Petroleum): Identify and evaluate potential drilling sites.

Facilities & Resources

Our PrE program provides access to specialized labs for core analysis, fluid properties, and drilling simulation. Students utilize industry-standard software for reservoir modeling and production optimization. Field trips to operational sites offer practical insights, and our faculty, with extensive industry experience, provide mentorship and opportunities for involvement in energy research initiatives.
