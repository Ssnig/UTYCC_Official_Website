Overview

The Information Science & Technology major is designed for students passionate about the intersection of **technology, data, and human interaction**. We provide a comprehensive foundation in key areas such as **software development**, **data analytics**, **cybersecurity**, and **user experience (UX) design**.

Our program emphasizes hands-on projects, collaborative learning, and real-world problem-solving, preparing you to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital landscape. You will learn to design, build, and manage complex information systems that drive innovation and organizational success.

Join us to become a leader in the digital transformation of tomorrow's world.
