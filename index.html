Student Rules & Regulations

1. Peaceful Learning Environment

  1. Students must not directly or indirectly disrupt or cause harm to the peaceful learning environment.

2. Prohibition of Moral Misconduct

  1. Students are strictly prohibited from using, possessing, buying, selling, or distributing narcotics. Smoking or chewing betel nut in classrooms, workshops, and campus premises is also forbidden.
  2. Students must not say or do anything that could harm the Rector, Pro-Rector, teachers, or staff.
  3. Students must not harass or harm female students or other women, on or off campus.
  4. Gambling, drinking alcohol, or using intoxicants are strictly prohibited on and off campus.
  5. Students must obey the instructions and guidance of their teachers.
  6. Students must not destroy, steal, or forcibly take the property of others, whether on or off campus.
  7. Fighting among students or with outsiders is not permitted, on or off campus.
  8. Students must adhere to all rules and regulations of the university/college/institute, including behavior and speech outside school hours.

3. Strict Adherence to School Discipline

  1. All students must attend classes regularly.
  2. To be eligible for the final exam, a student must have a minimum of **75% class attendance** throughout the academic year. Students with less than 75% attendance will not be allowed to take the final exam.
  3. Students must abide by the rules and regulations set by the school and for their specific courses.

4. Compliance with Exam Regulations

  1. All students must take the mid-term exam. If a student misses the mid-term exam, they will not be allowed to take the final exam.
  2. Cheating or using any dishonest means during an exam will result in severe action.

5. Dress Code and Conduct

  1. Students must wear the designated uniform and behave and speak in a manner appropriate for a student.

6. Simultaneous Enrollment

  1. While attending this university, students are not permitted to be simultaneously enrolled in any other university, college, or institute. Violation of this rule will result in the student being removed from the roster.

  2. Penalties for Violation

    Upon a thorough reading and understanding of the above-mentioned points, any failure to comply with or violation of these rules will result in disciplinary action appropriate to the severity of the offense.
