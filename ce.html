Computer Engineering (CE)

Program Overview

Computer Engineering (CE) is a discipline that integrates elements of computer science and electrical engineering. This program focuses on the design, development, and implementation of computer hardware and software systems. Students will gain a deep understanding of digital logic, computer architecture, embedded systems, operating systems, and network protocols, preparing them to innovate in a rapidly evolving technological landscape.

Career Opportunities

  • Hardware Engineer: Design and develop computer components.
  • Software Engineer: Develop applications and operating systems.
  • Embedded Systems Engineer: Design systems for specific functions (e.g., IoT devices).
  • Network Architect: Design and implement computer networks.
  • VLSI Design Engineer: Work on very large-scale integration circuits.

Facilities & Resources

Our CE program boasts cutting-edge labs for circuit design, embedded systems, and computer architecture. Students have access to specialized equipment for prototyping, testing, and debugging hardware, as well as high-performance computing resources for complex simulations. Faculty expertise spans various areas of computer engineering, providing ample opportunities for research collaboration.
