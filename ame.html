Advanced Materials Engineering (AME)

Program Overview

Advanced Materials Engineering (AME) is a dynamic field focusing on the design, analysis, and testing of aircraft, spacecraft, and other mechanical systems. This program combines principles of mechanical engineering with specialized aerospace topics such as aerodynamics, propulsion, flight mechanics, and structural design. Students are prepared to contribute to the next generation of aerospace innovation.

Career Opportunities

  • Aerospace Engineer: Design aircraft, spacecraft, and missiles.
  • Mechanical Engineer: Design and analyze mechanical systems.
  • Propulsion Engineer: Develop engines and propulsion systems.
  • Stress Analyst: Evaluate the structural integrity of components.
  • Manufacturing Engineer: Oversee the production of aerospace components.

Facilities & Resources

Our AME program offers access to advanced labs for aerodynamics (wind tunnels), materials testing, and robotics. Students utilize sophisticated CAD/CAE software for design and simulation. We provide opportunities for hands-on projects, including building and testing drones and small-scale aircraft. Faculty research areas include advanced materials, unmanned aerial vehicles, and sustainable aviation.
